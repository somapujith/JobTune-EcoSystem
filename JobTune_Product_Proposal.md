# JobTune - AI Career Acceleration Platform

## Vision

**Learn. Practice. Build. Optimize. Prepare. Get Hired.**

JobTune is an AI-powered career acceleration platform designed for
universities and students. It combines learning, project building,
career optimization, and placement preparation into one production-ready
ecosystem powered by Local LLMs.
